Safety

No hooks. No injection. Just pixels.

BDO ships with kernel-level anti-cheat, so this tracker is built around one non-negotiable rule: never touch the game.

It works exactly like a human watching the screen — it looks at the drop log you can already see and does the bookkeeping. Nothing reaches into the game itself.

—Reads the screen, nothing else

No memory reads, no DLL hooks, no packet capture, no process access. Capture is plain desktop screenshotting, the same as OBS.

—CPU-only while you play

All recognition runs on the CPU during sessions. Your GPU keeps rendering the game at full frames.

—Your data stays on your disk

Sessions live in a local SQLite file. The only network calls are market prices, community spot data, and update checks.

—Honest numbers by design

Uncertain reads are flagged in honey-yellow, never silently dropped — and never silently invented. High-value drops must be corroborated before they count.

FAQ

Questions grinders actually ask

Is this safe to use with BDO's anti-cheat?

The tracker never interacts with the game: no memory reading, no injection, no DLLs, no packet capture — it only takes screenshots of your own desktop and reads the pixels, the same capture method streaming software uses. It cannot see or touch the game process, and there's nothing for anti-cheat to detect inside the game.

Will it cost me FPS?

Capture uses lightweight desktop duplication, and every bit of recognition runs CPU-only while a session is live — the GPU stays fully on the game. It's built to track a 2-hour grind without you noticing it's there.

What do I need for it to work?

Windows 10 or 11, the game in 1920×1080 borderless windowed, and the drop log visible on screen. A one-time calibration points the tracker at your drop-log position, then it's set-and-forget.

What can it track?

Live grinding and hunting sessions (drop log + Get-All loot box reading), and cooking and alchemy sessions with full net-profit accounting. Alongside the live tracking there's a Materials planner for what you can craft from your storage, and a manual Enhancement ledger for what the RNG has cost you.

How accurate is it?

It is checked against the game, not against itself: recognition is tuned on real sessions where the before/after inventory was counted by hand, and production runs are matched item by item against that count — a 193-bottle alchemy brew came out exact on every line. It is also honest about uncertainty: a read it is not sure of is flagged in honey-yellow instead of hidden, and any drop worth over 1M silver needs a confident read or a second sighting before it enters your totals, so one blurry frame can never invent a jackpot. Unpriced and unrecognised drops are counted and shown, never silently dropped.

Something didn't count right — where do I report it?

The Discord. Say which spot and roughly when, and attach the file from Account → Export Diagnostics — it packs the frames around the miss into a ~10 MB zip with nothing else from your PC in it. That's usually enough to fix the read for that spot in the next release, for everyone.

Is it free?

Yes — free during the beta, and it stays free. It's a passion project built by a grinder who wanted real numbers; the only thing we ask is that you tell us when it's wrong.
